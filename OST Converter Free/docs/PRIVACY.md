# Webcent OST Converter Free Privacy Policy

*Draft template: have it reviewed before release. Last updated: 4 October 2026.*

Webcent Solutions ("we") makes Webcent OST Converter Free. This page explains what the app and our account server handle.

## What stays on your computer
- The Outlook files you open (OST/PST) and everything in them: messages, contacts, calendar, attachments.
- The converted output you save.
- Error reports. If the app hits an unexpected error it writes a text file to `%LOCALAPPDATA%\OST Converter\logs`
  (error type, message, stack trace, app and Windows version). **It is never sent automatically.** You may attach it
  to a support request.

**We never receive your mail content.** The conversion engine runs entirely on your device.

## What we receive (only if you create an account / sign in)
| Data | Why | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Email address | Creating your free account and signing you in with a one-time code | Our account server |
| One-time sign-in codes | Verifying it is you (short-lived, stored hashed) | Our account server |
| Device name (your PC's name) and an anonymous device identifier derived from your machine (not reversible to your hardware) | Enforcing the limit of two devices per account | Our account server |
| When you created the account, when you last signed in or refreshed, and how many times you signed in | Running the service, preventing abuse, support | Our account server |

We use your email address to run your account and to send the sign-in code. We do not sell it. We will not send marketing
email unless you separately agree to it.

The app contacts our server when you sign in, when it refreshes your sign-in (about once a day) and when you manage
devices. If you are offline it keeps working for up to 7 days on the last sign-in it received.

Sign-in codes are sent by email through our email provider (Resend), which therefore processes your email address and the code.

## Updates
When installed, the app checks our update site for new versions. The request is an ordinary web request (your IP
address is visible to the web host, as for any download); no account data is added to it.

## Storage and security
Your sign-in is stored on your PC encrypted with Windows data protection (tied to your Windows account). Account data on
our server is kept while your account exists and deleted when you ask us to delete it or after long inactivity.

## Your choices
Email Info@webcents.in to see, correct or delete your account data, or to remove a device. Uninstalling the app removes
the local sign-in file and logs.
